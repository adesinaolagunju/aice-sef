Five African Countries Where Technology Is Growing Rapidly

1. Nigeria - Africa's largest economy hosts a thriving startup ecosystem in Lagos, with fintech companies like Flutterwave and Paystack leading the way.
2. Kenya - Often called the "Silicon Savannah," Kenya pioneered mobile money with M-Pesa and has a vibrant tech hub scene in Nairobi.
3. South Africa - With advanced digital infrastructure and a strong venture capital market, South Africa is a leader in fintech, e-commerce and cloud services.
4. Egypt - A large young population and government-backed digital initiatives are driving fast growth in Cairo's startups and digital payments.
5. Rwanda - Through its Vision 2050 plan, Rwanda has invested heavily in broadband, smart-city projects and drone delivery of medical supplies.
